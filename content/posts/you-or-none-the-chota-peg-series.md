The young lady had decided that’s its either him or no one, the young man thought likewise. Affairs of the heart can be dangerous and may even alter the course of history. As they say had Cleopatras nose been an inch small the history of the world would have been different. Or imagine a face that launched a thousand ships .Many a bitter war, many a tragedy have emanated from the she or he at any cost or any price. Passion can be such a powerful mode of destruction. Sometimes it devours all the characters involved. No one is willing to take a step back and pacify and take a step back and recast his or her prejudices, jealousy and emotional involvement. But that’s how we are!!

After the chota peg!!
