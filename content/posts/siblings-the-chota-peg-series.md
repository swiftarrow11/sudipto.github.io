Relationship amongst siblings is a strange one. As little ones they are ever so attached. A while later they fight with each other on trivial issues but are willing to fight and protect each other from the outside world. With time this is one of those very few love affairs where the bonding is loosened considerably as they go on to set their own homes. Many however manage the healthy respect for each other. Sibling rivalry at is worst comes baring it’s claws with a no holds barred battle when the bone of contention is an inheritable asset. With respective spouses also throwing in their hats to fuel the fire,brothers and sisters who once swore for each other sometimes end in courts. And all the sweet childhood memories of living under one roof are well and truly brushed under the carpet and indulge in such bitterness that it would ashame strangers.

A close relationship which decays with time and material consideration. A strange one indeed. However it needs to be admitted that many do keep the attachment warm and healthy lifelong.
