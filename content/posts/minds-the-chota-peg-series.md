Taming , tempering and reining the ever wavering human mind which in a stream of consciousness jumps wildly from one to another completely unrelated subject and also amazingly unifies these opposing thoughts to justify uncivilised action ,is the purpose of all philosophy,religion and education .

AI the fourth industrial revolution of our times has. managed to predict with a great degree of accuracy human reaction to stimuli by machines but what about a mind which say equates a murder to patriotism or love to death. I and you are perfectly capable of thinking of simple normal matters in the most abnormal of ways. Years of civilisation, spirituality, rituals and education have not found an acceptable means of controlling the mind and teach it to think in straight lines.

After the chota peg!!!
